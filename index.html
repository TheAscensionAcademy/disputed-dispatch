Why It Actually Works

CFPB Complaints Are Not Regular Dispute Letters

Most credit repair businesses send dispute letters directly to bureaus. Bureaus can stall, rubber-stamp a "verified" response, or simply ignore a letter with zero consequences.

A CFPB complaint is different. It goes to a federal regulator with enforcement power. The bureau responds to the government on a public record admissible in litigation.

Companies that drag their feet on dispute letters respond within 15 days when a CFPB complaint is filed. Every time.

What is a 605B Letter?

Section 605B is a law inside the FCRA giving identity theft victims a direct right to block fraudulent information from their credit report. When submitted to a bureau with an FTC Identity Theft Report, the bureau must block every listed item within 4 business days — no investigation, no back-and-forth.

When Do You Need It?
  • 🔄
    After a CFPB complaint comes back "verified" — the 605B overrides that when identity theft is documented.
  • ⚡
    When you need faster results — CFPB takes 15 days. A 605B triggers a mandatory 4-day block. Use both together.
  • 🧩
    When rounds of disputes haven't worked — creates a permanent legal block bureaus must honor.
  • 📬
    As a first strike on clear-cut fraud — file CFPB complaint and 605B simultaneously for maximum pressure.

FAQ

Common Questions

How fast will complaints be filed?
Cases submitted before 4pm EST are filed same day. After 4pm, filed by 10am the next business day. You receive all confirmation numbers as each complaint is submitted.
Do you ever contact my client directly?
Never. We are strictly your back-end subcontractor. Everything goes to you — you deliver results to your client under your own brand. 100% invisible.
What format do I need to send the credit report in?
You don't need to send the PDF. Just list the disputed items in the intake form — company name, date, which bureaus, and the amount if applicable.
What's the difference between Bureau and Full Package?
Bureau Package files 3 complaints — one per bureau. Full Package adds up to 25 individual complaints against each specific lender, applying FCRA Section 604 pressure directly. For identity theft cases, always recommend Full Package.
What if a complaint comes back verified?
First-round filings resolve the majority of legitimate disputes. If an item survives, we provide escalation guidance and can file round-two complaints. Retainer clients get escalation included.
Is this legal for my credit repair business?
Yes. CFPB filing is a federally protected consumer right. You are subcontracting the filing process. You remain responsible for written authorization from your client, which CROA requires regardless of who files.
